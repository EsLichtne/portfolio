How I work

Principles I follow when building products

01

Product architecture

I figure out where data comes from, where it’s stored, who affects it and how the product’s entities connect, so the interface grows out of how the product actually works

02

Research

I design based on research into users’ needs, context and mental models, so the interface rests on data rather than assumptions about them

03

Design system

I treat the design system as the product’s working infrastructure: I build interfaces on it and extend it wherever new patterns, states and product tasks appear

04

Design engineering

I implement interfaces myself — from components to prototypes, screens and states. This shortens the path from decision to production and keeps the interface consistent from Figma to the user

About me

I came to product design from front-end development, so I see the interface from two sides at once: as a system of product decisions and as working code

I enjoy untangling complex systems and finding the form that makes them easy to use
